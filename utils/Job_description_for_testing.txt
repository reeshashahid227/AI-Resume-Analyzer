Data Scientist

We are looking for a Data Scientist to join our team.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets and identify meaningful business insights.
- Build and deploy machine learning models.
- Develop predictive models and statistical solutions.
- Clean, preprocess, and transform data.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and data visualization.
- Work with cross-functional teams to solve business problems.
- Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- 2+ years of experience in Data Science or Machine Learning.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Experience with Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and SQL.
- Knowledge of Machine Learning algorithms.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Matplotlib, Seaborn, or Tableau.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Good communication skills.

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with Deep Learning.
- Knowledge of TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure.
- Experience with NLP and Generative AI is a plus.
